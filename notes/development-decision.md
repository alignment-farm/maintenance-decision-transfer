# Development closure and frozen assessment decisions

Seed 601 is the only newly acquired development learner. At 512 acquisition
updates it passes 64/64 original and 32/32 development complete orders. Its
Novel/Bridged first revisions score 192/192 and 184/192 on characterization.
The four second-revision final endpoints are NN=144, NB=150, BN=120, BB=143.
No second revision preserves the complete task. No revision gives 168 and 160
on the current final policy, with all 24 newest obligations unmet in each state.
Both trained supports trade additional errors for the new obligation.

Apply the originally fixed fitting formulas without a new search. The developed
constant is Bridged. Both history rows also prefer Bridged, so the metadata and
nearest-pre-observation choices reduce to Bridged. The prefix fit's predicted
Bridged score exceeds Novel throughout the possible [0,1] prefix-score range:
the prefix control also reduces to Bridged. One development acquisition supplies
too little calibration diversity for these controls to make use of new signals.
This is known BEFORE assessment; fresh equality of their choices cannot show
that the observations contain no information. We retain those failed informative-
selection attempts and their costs rather than relabel them as useful predictors.

Raw prefix accuracy is not uniformly ordered like the endpoint: under Novel
history, Novel/Bridged prefixes score 3/16 and 8/16 and finish 144/192 and 150/192;
under Bridged history they score 8/16 and 4/16 but finish 120/192 and 143/192.
Simply choosing the more accurate short trial succeeds in one development
history and fails in the other. This is a concrete prefix-to-endpoint calibration
problem, not something supplied automatically by completion across examples.

The initial match-history claim, both constant supports, sparse direct validation
and full-condition direct validation remain distinct meaningful assessment
comparators. On the Novel development history both candidates tie 24/32 on full
validation although final scores differ by six; validation is not an oracle.
Prefix checkpoint + optimizer disk restoration exactly matches the uninterrupted
192-update endpoint adapter in the prespecified diagnostic.

The example table completes all 192 final orders; all 48 version/condition
checks pass. This supplies a substantive alternative and a clear action-set
limitation without enlarging the neural training campaign to seek a win.
We keep the original two untouched assessment acquisitions, 701/702, fixed
panels, acquisition ladder and supports. We do not add another development seed
or retune an observation model after seeing this diagnosis.

Original expectation remains in protocol/comparison-v1.md. Frozen predictor
coefficients and source-manifest hash are in evidence/frozen-predictors-v1.json.
Independent acquisition transfer remains to be assessed; development endpoints
are not counted as fresh evidence.
