# Independent maintenance-decision study

Read README.md first. This study owns methods, protocol, implementation, evidence,
resource sizing and publication for the commissioned bounded comparison.
Construct-2 owns theory and synthesis. Reuse sibling work with exact provenance;
do not modify the root or completed studies. No dependency on another study's
next result is required.

## Research practice

- Develop and execute the README's comparison autonomously within its bounded
  scope. Routine experimental choices and purposeful repairs require no further
  root approval. Explain consequential scope changes.
- Establish functioning acquisition and useful behavior. Finite gradients,
  verified updates and lower loss are mechanics, not successful learning.
- Preserve failed runs and acquisition attempts. Diagnose plausible acquisition
  failures through bounded distinguishing comparisons. Stop on explanation,
  demonstrated limitation or actual resource constraint; no neural win is required.
- Separate known history, pre-update observations, paid trials and counterfactual
  endpoint evaluation. Keep all descendants of an acquisition in one partition.
  Fresh evaluation must challenge claims developed during diagnosis.
- Compare new obligations, valid earlier behavior, losses and repaired errors.
  A superseded obligation is not something to preserve. Choosing the least bad
  candidate does not establish complete maintenance.
- Develop serious fixed-history, direct-validation and explicit-evidence controls.
  Disclose privileges, labels and addressing assumptions. Charge observation,
  training, fitting, use and repair; separate investigation from deployment costs.
- Read closest primary methods and implementations, recording exact paper
  versions and code revisions. Distinguish methods inspected, author-reported
  results, local adaptations and discovery leads. No broad novelty claim is needed.
- For arXiv discovery use the query API; cache responses, use a descriptive
  User-Agent, one connection and at most one request every three seconds across
  clients under our control. Honor Retry-After. Source/PDF retrieval is separate;
  preserve exact versions read.
- Keep evidence and reproduction proportional and inspectable. Publish concise
  findings and identifiable revisions. No separate manuscript is required.

avoid overlapping jobs that contend for memory or invalidate timings. Choose

training campaign merely to obtain a positive result.

supporting resources warrant it. Preserve claim-bearing artifacts in Git while
excluding secrets, environments, caches and base-model downloads.
